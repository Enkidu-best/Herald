Herald {v} - turns a book into an audiobook on this computer.

NARRATION
1. Drop a book into the window or click “Choose book” (txt, md, docx, epub,
   fb2, pdf). The book card shows right away how much sound there will be and
   how long to wait.
2. On the right - the voice, part length and format.
3. “Test the voice (1 min)” - a minute from the middle of the book: the sound
   starts as soon as it's ready, and the preview is saved as a file next to
   the book.
4. “How it will be read” - the text as the app will read it, with stress marks.
5. “Narrate book”. Parts appear in the list as they're ready - you can listen
   right away. “Pause”, “Stop”; after a stop - “Continue” from the same place.
   If another app is using the GPU, the window tells you and offers to wait.

READ ALOUD
Paste text (“From clipboard”, “Open file…”) and click “Read” - the sound starts
in a fraction of a second. The same button is “Pause” and “Continue” (or Space,
when the cursor is not in the text); ■ stops. The voice is on the right, along with tempo, pitch,
bass and brightness. The sentence being read is highlighted; click a sentence
to read from there. The disk icon saves what was read as an mp3, “×” clears the text.

VOICES
A clone is made from a recording of a narrator: “Add voice sample…” - the app
picks a clean passage by itself. All ready-made voices are here too, one per
row: ▶ - listen, “Download” (Vosk, Piper), “✎” - your own name for the lists,
“⋯” - hide or delete. On the right - the voice sound (tempo, pitch, bass,
brightness), “Listen”, “Save”.

SETTINGS
Engine and “Quality / speed” (“Faster”, “Balanced”, “Maximum quality”),
pauses, word check by sound, output device (“Same as system” - sound moves to
headphones by itself), narration for other apps, log.

MENU BOTTOM AND TOP
“Theme” - system, light or dark. Top right - the interface language RU | EN
and “Aa” - text size. “≡” next to the name collapses the menu.

LICENSES
Fine for commercial projects: the ESpeech engine, the Vosk and Piper voices; not
allowed: Silero and the main F5 engine (Misha) - non-commercial only. Cloning
someone's voice - only with the narrator's consent.

Everything runs on this computer; the internet is needed only for a one-time
download of models and voices.
