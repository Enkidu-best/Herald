Herald {v} - turn a book into an audiobook on this computer.

NARRATION
1. Drop a book into the window or click "Choose book" (txt, md, docx, epub,
   fb2, pdf); earlier books are under "Recent books". The book card shows at once
   how much audio there will be and how long to wait.
2. On the right - the voice ("Voice sound ›" - its tempo and timbre), part length
   and format: mp3 320 / 192 (standard) / 128 / 96 and 64 kbps (speech, mono) or FLAC.
3. "Test the voice (1 min)" - a minute from the middle of the book's longest part:
   sound starts as it is ready, the sample is saved next to the book.
4. "How it will be read" - the text as the program will read it, with stress marks.
   Disputed words are highlighted: click one to choose its stress for this book.
5. "Narrate book". Parts appear in the list as they are ready - you can listen
   right away. "Pause", "Stop"; after a stop - "Continue (k of N)" from the same
   place, "Start over" asks first. If another program is using the GPU, the window
   says so and offers "Wait until it's free".
6. "Service files" in the book card - stressed text, chunks, raw parts: "Open",
   "Clear". A finished book moves them to the trash by itself (Settings).

READ ALOUD
Paste text ("From clipboard", "Open file…") and click "Read" - sound starts in a
fraction of a second. The same button is "Pause" and "Continue" (or Space when the
cursor is not in the text); ■ stops. The voice is on the right; its sound is set on
the "Voices" page ("Voice sound ›"). The sentence being read is highlighted; click
a sentence to read from it. The floppy icon saves what was read as mp3, "×" clears.

VOICES
At the top - "My voices" (trained on the narrator's recordings), then Russian and
English voices, alphabetically. A clone is made from a narrator's recording: "Add voice
sample…" - the program picks a clean passage itself. Each voice is a row: ▶ -
listen, "Download" (Vosk, Piper), "✎" - your own name for the lists, "⋯" - hide /
show or delete (a clone goes to the trash and can be restored for 30 days). "Show
hidden" - see hidden voices too. On the right - the voice's sound (tempo, pitch,
bass, brightness), "Listen", "Save"; "More" - the voice's own weights, Qwen
sampling, noise reduction.

SETTINGS
Engine ("Main", "ESpeech", "Qwen ru-stress", "Draft") and "Quality / speed",
pauses, word check by sound, the book's service files, trash ("Restore", "Delete
forever"), audio output ("Same as system" - sound moves to headphones by itself),
clone voices in "Read aloud", narration for other programs, log.

BOTTOM OF THE MENU AND TOP
"Theme" - system, light or dark. Top right - interface language RU | EN and "Aa" -
text size 80-200 % (Cmd+= / Cmd+− / Cmd+0). "≡" next to the name collapses the menu.

LICENSES
Allowed in commercial projects: the ESpeech engine, Vosk and Piper voices; not
allowed: Silero and the main F5 engine (Misha) - non-commercial only. Cloning
someone else's voice - only with the narrator's consent; narrators' voices are for
personal use only.

Everything runs on this computer; the internet is needed only to download models
and voices once.
